Vael was a rogue hired by Ilyr to aid him in acquiring the Book of All, but he secretly planned to take it for another buyer; after copying Vael's plan and discovering the intended double-cross, Ilyr cast him into the sea as a liability.

Hidden beneath his erased identity, unknown to both Vael and the player until memories recovered or revealed in-story surface it: Vael once served as a knight in the royal guard before being dismissed, and the woman he loved was killed not long after. Grief and disgrace turned him into a sellsword, and that path eventually led Ilyr to hire him for the voyage to Eidolon.
