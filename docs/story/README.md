# Awash Story

## Premise

[Vael](../characters/vael.md), a dark elf thief, wakes alone on the shore of a forbidden island with no memory of his name, his past, or how he arrived. His survival and combat instincts remain intact, but he has never been to the island before.

Across ancient regions, safe settlements, and arcane strongholds, Vael races the wizard [Ilyr Voss](../characters/ilyr-voss.md) to the Book of All, a tome said to contain complete knowledge.

## Story Pillars

- Identity through choice: [Vael's past explains him without deciding who he becomes](theme.md).
- Memory as progression: regions and relics restore identity fragments alongside traversal abilities.
- Knowledge as danger: power without restraint corrupts both hero and antagonist.
- Place as narrative: every region reflects a past culture and its present collapse.
- Moral pressure: stopping the Book may require sacrifice.

## Main Cast

- [Vael](../characters/vael.md): Protagonist
- [Ilyr Voss](../characters/ilyr-voss.md): Antagonist
- [The Assassin](../characters/assassin.md): Conflicted ally
- [The Barbarian](../characters/barbarian.md): Rival

## Hidden Truth

Before the game, Ilyr hired Vael to steal the Book of All and brought the Assassin and Barbarian on the voyage. The Assassin helped conceal the expedition's purpose, while the Barbarian remained Ilyr's loyal enforcer.

Vael secretly planned to deliver the Book to another buyer. After copying Vael's plan and discovering the intended double-cross, Ilyr struck him with a powerful spell and cast him into the storm-dark sea. Believing Vael dead, the expedition continued to the island.

Vael survived, drifted to shore, and woke with his memory erased.

## Opening: Ashore Without a Name

Vael scavenges the shoreline, recovers [Blacktide](../items/equipment/weapons/blacktide.md) from the wreckage, and follows signs of the expedition inland. The opening establishes isolation, disorientation, and mistrust while teaching survival, movement, combat, and traversal.

## Amnesia As Progression

- New regions and relic encounters trigger memory fragments.
- Recovered memories reveal Vael's past choices and recontextualize allies, enemies, and motives.
- Each major memory is followed by a [mirrored present-day choice](theme.md#mirrored-choices).
- Vael's identity unfolds alongside gameplay unlocks without granting him a prior connection to the island.

## The Book Of All

The Book is bound beneath Blackreef Castle and contains complete knowledge, including forbidden dark arcane techniques. Ilyr believes it can impose permanent order, while the vault magnifies the motives of anyone attempting to claim it.

The final decision is left to the player:

- Destroy or reseal the Book.
- Bind the Book at personal cost.
- Divide its knowledge into guarded fragments.

## Chapter Outline

### Chapter 1: [Waking Coast](../setting/regions/waking-coast.md)

Vael wakes in the black surf with no name and only his instincts, then follows shattered oath-stones and signs of a recent landing toward Tidefen Village. The hostile coast teaches him to survive while Blacktide's silver edge reacts faintly to the memory-stripped carvings, suggesting the sword can cut more than steel. Tidefen's salvagers tell him that a young wizard named Ilyr has been buying charts to the inner island, and a memory-knot ritual restores a flash of Vael standing on a storm-tossed ship as someone behind him begins to cast a spell. Realizing his arrival was no accident, Vael follows Ilyr's trail into the jungle.

### Chapter 2: [Low Canopy Jungle](../setting/regions/low-canopy-jungle.md)

Vael crosses the Low Canopy's ruined medicine terraces, where altered predators guard reliefs of wardens carrying the Book of All inland, and descends through Smuggler Inlet to the Pirate Fort. There he discovers that Ilyr purchased a route to the island's deep vaults by granting the corsairs arcane protection. The Barbarian from Vael's fractured memories attacks on Ilyr's orders and calls Vael a dead man who should have stayed beneath the waves, but Vael survives and forces him to retreat. A memory echo from Ilyr's abandoned charts restores the expedition's true objective: they came to claim the Book, whose knowledge could unmake every limit placed upon magic.

### Chapter 3: [Underroot](../setting/regions/underroot.md)

Seeking a way past the island's sealed heights, Vael earns passage through divided Emberroot Village and descends into the memory-resonant Underroot caves, where old warding script repeats moments from his lost life. The Assassin shadows him through the caverns and covertly disables a trap, claiming only that Ilyr must not reach the vault yet. In the Relic Chamber, Vael rejects a vision of effortless power and awakens the double-jump sigil after Blacktide cuts through the trial's outer ward. The relic restores the full betrayal aboard the ship: Ilyr hired Vael to steal the Book, copied the thief's plan, and cast him into the sea after discovering Vael intended to take the prize for another buyer.

### Chapter 4: [High Canopy](../setting/regions/high-canopy.md)

Using the relic to reopen elevated routes, Vael climbs through Canopy Shaft into the suspended ruins of the High Canopy and pursues the Assassin along abandoned courier paths toward Observatory Ridge. Cornered beneath the celestial instruments, she admits that she helped Ilyr hide the expedition's purpose and stood aside during the betrayal, but now believes the Book will turn his desire for order into tyranny. Vael accepts her route through the observatory without granting forgiveness, and together they align its instruments to expose a path across the northern island. The activation broadcasts Blacktide's ward-cutting resonance, revealing Vael's survival to Ilyr and transforming the expedition from a secret race into an open hunt.

### Chapter 5: [Northern Reaches](../setting/regions/northern-reaches.md)

Vael crosses the storm-beaten North Shoals and reactivates the broken spans of the Floating Causeway while Ilyr's scouts and the Barbarian close in on him. At Glassmire Village, mirrored wetlands and layered oral histories resist the island's memory erosion, allowing the elders' chants to stabilize Vael's recovered fragments. He remembers that he was a thief hired to breach the island's wards and steal the Book, and that he planned to betray Ilyr by delivering it to another buyer. When the Barbarian assaults Glassmire rather than accept evidence of Ilyr's treachery, the Assassin openly turns against Ilyr and commits herself to stopping him, though neither she nor Vael pretends that her choice erases what she did.

### Chapter 6: [Stormwatch Tower](../setting/regions/stormwatch-tower.md)

Vael and the Assassin assault Stormwatch Tower before Ilyr can finish projecting lock-breaking frequencies toward Blackreef, fighting upward through weather engines and unstable ritual chambers while the Barbarian defends his master's work. Vael defeats the Barbarian but refuses to kill him, leaving him with evidence that Ilyr always intended to sacrifice every member of the expedition once the vault opened. At the tower's summit, Vael claims the arcane tuning key and confronts Ilyr through a ritual projection. Ilyr argues that complete knowledge is the only cure for chaos and exposes Vael's original motive for joining the voyage: he accepted the job for profit and intended to double-cross everyone, making his present pursuit more than revenge and forcing him to confront how closely his own ambition once resembled Ilyr's.

### Chapter 7: [Blackreef Approach](../setting/regions/blackreef-approach.md)

With the arcane key, Vael passes through Ironwake Village and rallies a limited defense before crossing the exhausted battlements of the Castle Approach. Ilyr drives pirates, mercenaries, and arcane constructs against the prison-temple, sacrificing his own forces to strip away its outer seals, while Ironwake prepares to burn rather than let the breach spread west. The Barbarian returns wounded and finally turns on Ilyr after seeing the promised new order begin with the disposal of its most loyal servant. He holds the collapsing approach against Ilyr's remaining forces so Vael and the Assassin can enter Blackreef, a costly act that rejects his master without excusing the people he harmed in that master's name.

### Chapter 8: [Blackreef Castle](../setting/regions/blackreef-castle.md)

Vael and the Assassin cross Blackreef Castle's doctrine halls and weaponized memory rites, where each chamber tests whether their motives differ from Ilyr's hunger for control, and descend to the Book Vault as Ilyr breaks its final outer seal. Blacktide cuts the inner ward, but the vault magnifies both men's deepest justifications: Ilyr sees perfect order achieved through absolute knowledge, while Vael confronts the greed and distrust that once led him to plan his own betrayal. After defeating Ilyr, Vael reaches the Book of All with his identity restored but no command from his former self. The final decision belongs to the person he has become: destroy or reseal the Book, bind it to himself at personal cost, or divide its knowledge into guarded fragments so no single will can possess it all.
