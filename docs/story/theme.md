# Story Theme

## Central Theme

**Your past explains you, but it does not decide who you become.**

Vael's recovered memories reveal that he was a thief who joined Ilyr's expedition for profit and intended his own betrayal. The story does not erase or excuse those choices; it asks whether Vael will repeat them after learning who he was.

## Mirrored Choices

Each major memory should reveal a selfish or harmful choice from Vael's past. Soon afterward, the present-day story should confront him with a similar decision whose outcome is determined by the player.

Examples:

- A memory of taking payment before people -> keep a reward or help without profit.
- A memory of planning to betray Ilyr -> betray a new ally or honor the agreement.
- A memory of treating knowledge as property -> conceal useful knowledge or share it.
- A memory of eliminating risks -> kill a defeated rival or show restraint.

## Consequences

- Choices affect NPC trust, dialogue, and available support.
- Consequences appear through character reactions and later situations, not a visible morality meter.
- No single choice defines Vael; the pattern across the journey matters.
- The final Book decision reflects the person shaped by those choices without declaring one history inevitable.

## Narrative Rule

Recovered memories establish what Vael did, while present-day choices establish who he becomes.
