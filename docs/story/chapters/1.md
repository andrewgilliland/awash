Vael wakes in the black surf with no name and only his instincts, where Tidefen salvager Mara Venn finds him beside the wreckage holding Blacktide. She guides him toward Tidefen Village while the hostile coast teaches him to survive and Blacktide's silver edge reacts faintly to the memory-stripped carvings, suggesting the sword can cut more than steel. Mara tells him that a young wizard named Ilyr has been buying charts to the inner island, then ties a memory knot that restores a flash of Vael standing on a storm-tossed ship as someone behind him begins to cast a spell. Realizing his arrival was no accident, Vael follows Ilyr's trail into the jungle.
