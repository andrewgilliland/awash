Vael wakes in the black surf with no name and only his instincts. Tidefen salvager Mara Venn finds him beside the wreckage clutching Blacktide and offers to guide him toward her village.

They cross beaches littered with broken hulls, wade through tidal pools between jagged rocks, and climb salt-slick ledges above the waves. Scavengers hunt in the shallows, storm wisps gather around exposed heights, and shattered runes mark the only safe path inland. The journey forces Vael to trust his forgotten combat instincts while Mara reads the tides and keeps them ahead of the rising water.

At the end of the coast, the Nameless Warden blocks the rune road after Ilyr's scouts disturbed its ancient watch. The armored spirit treats Vael as another intruder until Blacktide cuts through its magical guard, proving the sword can bite into wards and confirming that the expedition forced its way inland. After its defeat, the Warden yields and opens the road to Tidefen.

In the village, Mara tells Vael that a young wizard named Ilyr has been buying charts to the inner island. She shows him a salvaged rune she has kept since finding it near the wreck, and its touch restores a flash of Vael standing on a storm-tossed ship as someone behind him begins to cast a spell. Realizing his arrival was no accident, Vael follows Ilyr's trail into the jungle.
