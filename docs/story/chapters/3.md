Seeking a way past the island's sealed heights, Vael earns passage through divided Emberroot Village and descends into the memory-resonant Underroot caves, where old warding script repeats moments from his lost life. Mira shadows him through the caverns and covertly disables a trap, claiming only that Ilyr must not reach the vault yet. In the Relic Chamber, Vael rejects a vision of effortless power and awakens the double-jump sigil after Blacktide cuts through the trial's outer ward. The relic restores the full betrayal aboard the ship: Ilyr hired Vael to steal the Book, copied the thief's plan, and cast him into the sea after discovering Vael intended to take the prize for another buyer.
