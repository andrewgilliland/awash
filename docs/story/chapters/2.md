Vael crosses the Low Canopy's ruined medicine terraces, where altered predators guard reliefs of wardens carrying the Book of All inland, and climbs down a giant hollow root toward Smuggler Inlet. Where the root opens above the Inlet, Rusk, the barbarian from Vael's fractured memories, ambushes him on Ilyr's orders, shuts him in with roots grown from his maul's arcane brand, and calls Vael a dead man who should have stayed beneath the waves, but Vael survives and forces him to retreat. Vael descends through Smuggler Inlet to the Pirate Fort, where he discovers that Ilyr purchased a route to the island's deep vaults by granting the corsairs arcane protection. A memory echo from Ilyr's abandoned charts restores the expedition's true objective: they came to claim the Book, whose knowledge could unmake every limit placed upon magic.
