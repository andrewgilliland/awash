# Enemy List

Enemies and bosses are grouped by the regions defined in [setting.md](../story/setting.md).

## Size Classes

- **Small**: Occupies less space than Vael.
- **Medium**: Roughly comparable to Vael's height and combat footprint.
- **Large**: Substantially larger than Vael and built around broad attacks.
- **Colossal**: An arena-scale creature or construct that dominates the environment.

## Waking Coast

### Enemies

- **Wraith**: A spectral oathkeeper that phases through attacks until its exposed oath-stone anchor is struck.
  **Visual:** A translucent figure in rotted ceremonial robes trails seawater around a glowing stone lodged in its chest.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Scavenger**: An amphibious pack hunter that darts through shallows and drags prey toward coastal hazards.
  **Visual:** This hunched, slick-skinned creature wears shells and fishing scraps across its long forearms and hooked claws.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Storm Wisp**: A volatile memory echo that rides sea winds and releases short arcs of lightning when approached.
  **Visual:** A fist-sized knot of pale mist flickers with blue lightning and leaves a ribbon of rain in its wake.
  **Size:** Small.

### Boss

- **Nameless Warden**: A guardian stripped of its identity by the Memory Tempests, testing Vael with shield counters and oath-stone shockwaves before allowing passage to Tidefen.
  **Visual:** A tall armored sentinel bears a blank stone mask, a salt-crusted shield, and a cloak unraveled into wet cords.
  **Size:** Large.

## Low Canopy Jungle

### Enemies

- **Stalker**: An altered jungle predator that leaps between platforms and marks landing zones with grasping roots.
  **Visual:** A lean six-legged cat has bark-plated shoulders, vine tendons, and thorny roots unfurling from its paws.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Spore Beast**: A swollen garden beast that vents toxic spores when struck, forcing Vael to keep changing position.
  **Visual:** A squat boar-like animal lumbers beneath bright fungal shelves that pulse across its bloated green back.
  **Size:** Large.
- **Pirate**: A pirate skirmisher who alternates cutlass rushes with bombs thrown from guarded ledges.
  **Visual:** A weathered corsair wears patched storm leathers, a brass half-mask, and a belt crowded with clay bombs.
  **Size:** Medium.

### Boss

- **The Barbarian**: Ilyr's relentless enforcer uses armored charges, heavy guard breaks, and ground-shaking blows before Vael forces him to retreat.
  **Visual:** A broad warrior in scarred hide and scavenged plate carries an oversized maul etched with a single arcane brand.
  **Size:** Large.

## Underroot

### Enemies

- **Sentinel**: A stone construct that travels between inscribed walls and seals portions of the arena with warding lines.
  **Visual:** A blocky humanoid of dark stone is divided by luminous runes that continue across nearby walls when it moves.
  **Size:** Large.
- **Echo**: A memory remnant that repeats Vael's previous attack after a delay, punishing predictable rhythms.
  **Visual:** A blurred silhouette copies Vael's outline in layered afterimages, each one lagging a heartbeat behind the last.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Burrower**: A plated cave beast that tunnels beneath the floor and erupts in showers of falling stone.
  **Visual:** A low, eyeless reptile is covered in overlapping mineral plates and crowned by a wedge-shaped digging horn.
  **Size:** Large.

### Boss

- **Relic Guardian**: The chamber's ancient keeper changes its defenses in response to aggression, testing Vael's intent before awakening the double-jump sigil.
  **Visual:** A floating suit of ceremonial armor surrounds an empty core where the double-jump sigil burns like a small star.
  **Size:** Large.

## High Canopy

### Enemies

- **Hunter**: A blind predator that tracks noise, dives from overhead, and grapples anyone who remains still too long.
  **Visual:** A pale winged beast clings upside down with folded limbs, its eyeless face split by a wide listening frill.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Courier**: A spectral scout that races along old zipline routes and drops traps onto lower platforms.
  **Visual:** A translucent runner in light scout armor hangs from a spectral pulley with satchels streaming behind it.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Anchor**: An arcane parasite that tethers Vael to floating ruins and restricts his aerial movement until destroyed.
  **Visual:** A black metal seed floats inside a ring of roots, casting taut violet threads toward nearby stone.
  **Size:** Small.

### Boss

- **Observatory Sentinel**: A many-limbed celestial construct that rotates platforms and gravity fields while guarding the instruments the assassin helps Vael align.
  **Visual:** A brass and white-stone figure unfolds six instrument arms around a rotating glass model of the night sky.
  **Size:** Colossal.

## Northern Reaches

### Enemies

- **Crawler**: A shelled tidal predator that hides beneath flooded ground and attacks as the water recedes.
  **Visual:** A wide crustacean with mirrored shell plates and needle legs lies almost flush beneath the wet sand.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Gale**: A wind spirit that creates directional gusts capable of pushing Vael from exposed platforms.
  **Visual:** A twisting column of gray air carries feathers, salt crystals, and a faint mask-like face within its center.
  **Size:** Large.
- **Drifter**: A floating construct vulnerable only during brief alignment windows between its anti-gravity anchors.
  **Visual:** Three stone rings orbit a faceted core, separating and realigning as it glides above the causeway.
  **Size:** Medium.

### Boss

- **Beacon Keeper**: The last automated keeper of North Shoals commands lightning rods and sweeping beacon rays, releasing the causeway controls when defeated.
  **Visual:** A weathered bronze automaton wears a lighthouse lens as its head and trails grounding chains from its long arms.
  **Size:** Large.

## Stormwatch Tower

### Enemies

- **Wardbreaker**: An Ilyr-trained mage who destabilizes platforms and defensive wards with interruptible arcane pulses.
  **Visual:** A hooded mage in fitted gray robes carries a forked tuning staff and glowing seal fragments around both wrists.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Husk**: A weather engine given motion, storing lightning from nearby hazards before discharging it across the floor.
  **Visual:** A barrel-shaped machine walks on four iron legs beneath a cracked glass chamber boiling with storm clouds.
  **Size:** Large.
- **Thrall**: A bound soldier who strengthens nearby enemies through chants that can be broken with sustained pressure.
  **Visual:** A rigid fighter in plain armor has silver ritual wire stitched from the jaw to a glowing collar.
  **Size:** Medium.

### Boss

- **The Barbarian**: Empowered by Stormwatch's engines, Ilyr's enforcer adds lightning trails and arcane armor to his earlier attacks until Vael defeats but spares him.
  **Visual:** His scarred plate is fused with blue conduits, while stormlight crawls over his maul and raises his hair like a burning crown.
  **Size:** Large.

## Blackreef Approach

### Enemies

- **Mercenary**: A hired soldier who advances behind a shield and calls ranged support when allowed to hold ground.
  **Visual:** A practical fighter in mismatched campaign armor carries a rectangular tower shield marked with Ilyr's temporary sigil.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Fallen Warden**: A broken initiate whose unstable oath releases disorienting memory bursts whenever its guard is shattered.
  **Visual:** Cracked ceremonial armor hangs from a gaunt figure whose face cycles through the fading features of former wardens.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Lockbreaker**: One of Ilyr's siege constructs, firing seal-breaking beams that also awaken dormant castle traps.
  **Visual:** A squat iron engine braces on six legs and aims a bright tuning crystal from within a cage of copper forks.
  **Size:** Large.

### Boss

- **Warden Marshal**: An ancient defender that treats every army as an invader, commanding battlement traps and spectral formations amid Ilyr's siege.
  **Visual:** A towering commander in black ceremonial plate wears a crown-like helm and carries a banner made from solid ghostlight.
  **Size:** Large.

## Blackreef Castle

### Enemies

- **Keeper**: A prison-temple enforcer that creates silence fields where Vael cannot use ranged abilities.
  **Visual:** A severe robed figure wears a sealed bronze visor and carries chained tablets covered in censored lines.
  **Size:** Medium.
- **Leech**: A parasitic echo that steals one of Vael's recent actions and turns it against him until interrupted.
  **Visual:** A translucent ribbon of flesh and script coils through the air around a mouth shaped like an open eye.
  **Size:** Small.
- **Shade**: A manifestation of forbidden technique that imitates Vael's combat style with stronger but clearly telegraphed attacks.
  **Visual:** A glossy black double of Vael has no features except thin white lines tracing each attack before it moves.
  **Size:** Medium.

### Boss

- **Ilyr Voss**: The expedition's architect combines ritual spell sequences, weaponized memories, and shifting vault seals in his final attempt to claim the Book of All.
  **Visual:** A young wizard in torn expedition robes stands within orbiting pages and seal fragments, his eyes lit by more knowledge than his body can contain.
  **Size:** Medium.
