# Testing: The game and its UI

Starting the game, the Pause menu, music, dialogue and Interact; the Run; and the end-to-end playtests. How to run them is in [Testing](../testing.md).

- The game starts at the start screen, and the first Room loads with its player, world, and pause menu.
- The Pause menu opens on its first Panel (every time), shows a Panel when its button takes focus, moves from Panel to Panel with left and right (wrapping, and with A and D as well as the arrows), keeps the Panel shown on going down to Resume and comes back up to its button, resumes play, pairs every button with a Panel and needs no Back, disables and passes over a button with no Panel, fits the 160x144 screen, repeats no tab's name as a title in its Panel, starts every Panel's content at the top, draws all text in the compact pixel font while keeping the menu's styles, and names the Room's region on the Map, and keeps Rooms past the Map's edges inside its Panel (`tests/pause_menu_tests.gd`, run after one frame so the menu is in the tree). The Map opens on the whole World, which fits its Panel with every screen at least 6 by 5 pixels; Jump zooms it in and Attack out a step at a time, stopping at each end, only while the Map's button has focus; it keeps its zoom when opened again; and the Map key (Tab) opens the Pause menu on the Map and closes it, and turns to the Map from another Panel, but does nothing while the Shop holds play paused (`tests/map_zoom_tests.gd`). Interact on the Map's button starts exploring the Map and again stops, and does nothing away from it; exploring, the directions pan the Map and leave the menu where it is, the same Map distance at every zoom, not at all at World zoom, and no further than the World's edge; zooming or leaving keeps the pan; the menu opened again is centred on the Player, not exploring; and the Map key still closes the menu (`tests/map_pan_tests.gd`).
- The start screen shows its animated logo and a blinking PRESS START, a start press opens the menu on START, moving between options ticks, palms frame its beach, and its theme loops on the `Music` bus (`tests/start_screen_tests.gd`); a playtest waits for the theme, presses START from a Run left in the palm shore, hears the theme fade, and arrives in a fresh Run at the first Room's start Entry. With a Saved Run the menu opens on CONTINUE, which takes it up with no intro, and START asks first, where NO or cancel goes back, pause does nothing, and YES begins a new Run on the intro (`tests/start_screen_save_tests.gd`).
- The Saved Run holds every part of the Run but the intro and reads back the same; one of another version, missing a part, mistyping one or saved in a Room the World no longer holds is refused and leaves the Run as it was; it is written beside its file and renamed over it; one that can't be read is no Saved Run and is left in place; and a Run saved as the Player falls wakes where a defeat would (`tests/saved_run_tests.gd`). A test run keeps its Saved Run apart from the player's own, and the Run saves on entering a Room, ringing a Rest point, Quit to Desktop and closing the window (`tests/saving_tests.gd`). Playtests: the Player quits in Tidefen with some Gold and CONTINUE puts them back at the Entry they came in by, Gold and all; START over a Saved Run asks, NO goes back, and YES begins anew on Mara's intro (`tests/saving_playtests.gd`).
- The Music autoload plays a track on the `Music` bus, carries on when asked for the track already playing, switches tracks and stops, and a Room starts its Region's theme when it is ready (`tests/music_tests.gd`); the walk-between-Rooms playtest checks that the Waking Coast theme plays on through the Exit without starting over.
- Dialogue types each line out, a press finishes a line or moves to the next, it ends after its last line, and a line can carry a cue (`tests/dialogue_tests.gd`); the dialogue box shows the speaker and line, holds the Player still and calls out each line's cue as it opens, Mara stands in the first Room with a talk zone drawn from her sheet and walks in from just off the screen Vael wakes on, a fresh Run is not owed the intro, every line fits the box, its text is in the compact pixel font, and Mara hands Vael his sword on one line (`tests/intro_tests.gd`). The box sits at the foot of the screen, moves to the top when the foot would cover the Player or the speaker, stays at the foot when the top would cover one too, moves its Next arrow with it, and keeps its side until the conversation ends; a Villager and Mara each keep themselves in view (`tests/dialogue_box_placement_tests.gd`). Playtests: a new game from START plays the intro, Blacktide leaves the sand, and control comes back; Interact talks to Mara again; in both the box is clear of Vael and Mara; waking after a defeat has no intro.
- An Interactable sits on the `trigger` layer watching the Player; Interact uses the nearest one in reach, does nothing out of reach or while the Player is held still, its prompt shows only while the Player can act, and Mara is reached through one (`tests/interaction_tests.gd`). A Villager is reached through one too; Interact opens its lines in its own name in the Dialogue box, in order, and it turns to face whoever talks to it, idling on the two frames of its sheet (`tests/villager_tests.gd`).
- Every `res://` path written in the game's scenes, resources, scripts, shaders, tests and project settings names a file that exists, so a moved or deleted asset never leaves a reference behind (`tests/asset_path_tests.gd`). Move assets with `scripts/tools/move_asset.py`, which takes each asset's `.import` file along and rewrites every mention of its path.
- The Run begins in `waking_coast_1` at its `start` Entry, enters Rooms at Entries, starts over, and ignores empty ids (`tests/run_state_tests.gd`).
- The Player appears at the Run's Entry in the first Room.
- The deterministic main-scene spawn puts the Player on the default Entry, over a `WorldLayer` with tiles.
- End-to-end playtests (`tests/playtest_runner.gd`, running on the input, frame and movement helpers in `tests/playtest_harness.gd`, with the coast's walks, the fights and the Run's story in `tests/coast_playtests.gd`, `tests/fight_playtests.gd` and `tests/run_playtests.gd`, each extending the one before it) play the real first Room, with autoloads, input action events and physics frames at `--fixed-fps 60`: the Player walks right, jumps and lands, hops lower for a tap of jump than for a jump held to the top, plays an attack to its end and back to idle, pause freezes and resumes play, and left and right page the Pause menu from Stats on to Inventory and round to Map.
