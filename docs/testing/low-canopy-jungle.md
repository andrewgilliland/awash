# Testing: The Low Canopy Jungle

- The Low Canopy Jungle is a Region of its own, east of Tidefen, its sheets cut from the jungle mockup (`assets/tilesets/jungle_1.aseprite`) rather than a pack: its grass, earth and stone walls solid, its mossy ledges, plank decks and rope bridge one-way, its pools the coast's shallow water, a Background of sky, clouds, far trunks and thicket whose layers scroll across but hold their height on the screen, and a looping theme of its own; the Shrinekeeper Camp, its Safe spoke, is a Region of its own that keeps its sheets and Background (`tests/low_canopy_jungle_region_tests.gd`). Its Rooms each lay out their landmarks and are walked with real input (`tests/jungle_playtests.gd`): the harbour trail (`low_canopy_jungle_1`), from a shore level with Tidefen's landing up a mossy ledge and past a waterfall into its pool, onto a bank and over a root step to the terraces; and the lower terraces (`low_canopy_jungle_2`), whose medicine beds climb east behind stone walls a jump at a time, a plank deck from the top bed west to a terrace with a ruined relief of wardens carrying the Book, and a plank deck over its top bed east to the canopy walk, the floor over the Spore Beast's lair shut until that Room is built; the canopy walk (`low_canopy_jungle_3`), between two treehouse decks, its rope bridge broken in the middle, railed over each half, jumped across either way, over a forest floor a fall climbs back up from to either deck; the upper terraces (`low_canopy_jungle_4`), three screens of beds and mossy ledges a jump apart, walled where it has no Exit, and high on its west side a chest of two Mourning Salts on a shelf no single jump reaches, though a double one would; and the shrine steps (`low_canopy_jungle_6`), worn stone steps up to the camp's gate, drawn barred, walled behind, with no Exit east yet and a Sign that says so (`tests/low_canopy_jungle_room_1_tests.gd` to `tests/low_canopy_jungle_room_6_tests.gd`, sharing `tests/jungle_room_checks.gd`). Played with real input, the Player leaps the bridge's gap at a run, climbs the upper terraces a jump at a time to the shrine steps, and reads the camp's gate (`tests/jungle_playtests.gd`). Every Room draws from the packs, or from art drawn for Awash: Tidefen's bell and the jungle's sheets (`tests/every_room_tests.gd`).
