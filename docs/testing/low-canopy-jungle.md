# Testing: The Low Canopy Jungle

- The Low Canopy Jungle is a Region of its own, east of Tidefen, its sheets cut from the jungle mockup (`assets/tilesets/jungle_1.aseprite`) rather than a pack: its grass, earth and stone walls solid, its mossy ledges and plank decks one-way, its pools the coast's shallow water, a Background of sky, clouds, far trunks and thicket whose layers scroll across but hold their height on the screen, and a looping theme of its own; the Shrinekeeper Camp, its Safe spoke, is a Region of its own that keeps its sheets and Background (`tests/low_canopy_jungle_region_tests.gd`). Its Rooms each lay out their landmarks and are walked with real input (`tests/jungle_playtests.gd`): the harbour trail (`low_canopy_jungle_1`), from a shore level with Tidefen's landing up a mossy ledge and past a waterfall into its pool, onto a bank and over a root step to the terraces; and the lower terraces (`low_canopy_jungle_2`), whose medicine beds climb east behind stone walls a jump at a time, a plank deck from the top bed west to a terrace with a ruined relief of wardens carrying the Book, and its east end and the floor over the Spore Beast's lair shut until those Rooms are built (`tests/low_canopy_jungle_room_1_tests.gd`, `tests/low_canopy_jungle_room_2_tests.gd`). Every Room draws from the packs, or from art drawn for Awash: Tidefen's bell and the jungle's sheets (`tests/every_room_tests.gd`).
