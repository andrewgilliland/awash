# Testing: The Player and combat

The Player's controller, rig and body; Health, Levels, Supplies and Equipment; and each enemy and Boss, their hits and sounds. How to run them is in [Testing](../testing.md).

- The Player controller follows each Movement and States rule in `docs/specs/player-spec.md` (`tests/player_controller_tests.gd`).
- The Player rig follows each Animation rule, and its table is complete: a row for every state and one sword offset per sword frame (`tests/player_rig_tests.gd`).
- The player scene wires the rig: its sprites follow controller state and facing, driven through the body's `controller`.
- Health starts full, or at a carried amount, takes hits, blocks them while invulnerable, stops at zero, and raises its maximum and what is left together, except when defeated (`tests/health_tests.gd`). The Run carries the Player's Health and Experience between Rooms, and a defeat keeps the Run and its Experience, back at the start with full Health (`tests/run_state_tests.gd`).
- The Level table starts at no Experience and rises in tens; a new Run is Level 1, each Level gives its row's stats, a Level is reached exactly at its threshold, one gain can raise several, Experience stops raising Levels at the highest, counts down to the next, and can start from a carried total (`tests/level_tests.gd`). The Player starts at the Run's Level, Experience raises its Level and Health and carries in the Run, Strength adds to quick and Charged hits, reaching a Level chimes and shows LEVEL UP, kept on screen at a Room's edge, while Experience short of one stays quiet (`tests/player_growth_tests.gd`). Each enemy kind is worth its own Experience, reports its defeat once with it, and a Room hands that to the Player; each kind has its own drop chance, a lucky roll leaves a Brinecap where the enemy fell on the Room's next physics step and an unlucky one leaves nothing, and a Brinecap rests on the world layer and watches for the Player (`tests/enemy_experience_tests.gd`).
- Supplies start empty, name each kind and what it restores, add one at a time up to 9, spend one at a time, never below none, list the kinds carried, and start from the Run's counts (`tests/supplies_tests.gd`). A new Run wears the Worn Leather Tunic, which gives no Defense; Defense takes its value off a hit, a hit it lowers still costs at least 5, a weaker hit is never raised, a new Run owns only the tunic, taking Armor off leaves no Defense, only owned Armor can be worn, Equipment starts from what the Run carried, unknown Armor is neither owned nor worn, every Armor has an icon, a new Run holds the Short Sword as its only Weapon, every Weapon has an icon, and the Defense with any Armor, or none, can be read (`tests/equipment_tests.gd`). The Player wears the Run's Armor, the tunic leaves a spear hit at 10, Defense takes its value off each hit the Player takes, taking Armor off and putting it on goes into the Run, and the Player can start with it off (`tests/player_armor_tests.gd`); the Equipment Panel shows the Weapon and Armor rows above a divider, lists the focused slot's pieces below it with the equipped one marked and None first for Armor, runs down from Armor to Resume and back, moves into a slot's pieces when it is chosen with the slot lit and back out from the top, takes the Armor off with None and puts it back on, changes nothing when the equipped piece is chosen, previews the Defense while an Armor is focused, and describes the focused slot's piece or the focused piece in a two-line strip at its foot (`tests/equipment_panel_tests.gd`); every Weapon, Armor and kind of Supply has a description that wraps into two 35-character lines (`tests/description_tests.gd`); the Run carries the Armor owned and worn through a defeat (`tests/run_state_tests.gd`). Health heals up to its maximum but not from defeat (`tests/health_tests.gd`), and the Run carries Supplies through Rooms and a defeat (`tests/run_state_tests.gd`). The Player starts with the Run's Supplies, collects a Brinecap with a sound and into the Run, stops at 9, eats one for 10 Health with a chime, keeps it at full Health, and does nothing with none, held still or defeated (`tests/player_supplies_tests.gd`). The Inventory lists each kind as a row, name left and count right, or says none are carried; down from its tab reaches each row, then Resume, and back up; choosing a Brinecap eats it with play still paused and the eat sound playing through the pause; at full Health it keeps it and says `Health is full` in the description strip's place, in yellow, until the note is done; a two-line strip at the Panel's foot describes the focused kind; eating the last one returns focus to the tab (`tests/inventory_tests.gd`).
- The Health bar fills to the share of Health left and shows nothing before it has read it. A hit drops the fill at once while the lost Health drains away after it. It hides with no Player, sits on screen in the top-left corner, and sits under every Room's Pause menu. Stats shows the Level, Experience toward the next (or MAX), Health left out of its total, Strength and Defense, and a defeated Player stops answering the controls (`tests/health_bar_tests.gd`).
- The Scavenger brain patrols, winds up only when the Player is near and level, lunges then recovers, is knocked back by a hit and stays still once defeated (`tests/scavenger_brain_tests.gd`).
- The Player controller is knocked back by a hit, ignores input while stunned and loses an attack to a hit (`tests/player_controller_tests.gd`).
- The Player and Scavenger carry their Hurtboxes and Hitboxes on the right layers, the sword is live only during an Attack, a hit costs the Player Health and knocks it away, Health counts in tens (50 for Vael, 30 for a Scavenger, 20 for a Storm Wisp), hits differ in strength (a spear 10, an arc 15, a quick Attack 10, a Charged Attack 20), a full Charge makes the sword blink, and the palm shore keeps a Scavenger in its pool (`tests/combat_scene_tests.gd`).
- Playtests: the sword defeats the Scavenger, earning its Experience; with the Room's roll fixed to drop, a defeated Scavenger leaves a Brinecap that falls to the ground, the Player walks onto it to collect it, and, hurt, pauses, moves right to the Inventory, down to the Brinecap, and chooses it to eat it back to 10 more Health; and its lunge hurts the Player. A defeated Player falls and won't move, then wakes in the first Room with full Health and its Run kept. A hit taken in one Room is still taken after an Exit, and the Health bar shows it.
- Landing after a real fall and starting an Attack make sounds, other state changes and a moment in the air are silent, and the controller reports a jump but not a walk off a ledge (`tests/player_sounds_tests.gd`, `tests/player_controller_tests.gd`).
- The Storm Wisp brain drifts between its bounds and bobs at its height, crackles when the Player comes near, arcs, then rests, and loses its arc to a hit while it crackles (`tests/storm_wisp_brain_tests.gd`). Its sounds follow its state changes (`tests/storm_wisp_sounds_tests.gd`). Its scene flies through the world with a hurtbox and a circular arc that is live only while it arcs, is drawn from its sheet, and carries positional sounds; one rides the wind over the jungle trail's heights within sword reach (`tests/storm_wisp_scene_tests.gd`). Playtests wait out its arc and disperse it with the sword, disperse it with one Charged Attack, and stand in its arc to be hurt for 15.
- The Wraith brain drifts phased across its haunt and bobs at its height, drifts toward a Player in sight without leaving its haunt, manifests facing a near Player, lashes, exposes its Rune anchor, then stays phased a while before it can manifest again, and phases again when its anchor is struck (`tests/wraith_brain_tests.gd`). Its sounds follow its state changes (`tests/wraith_sounds_tests.gd`). Its scene floats through the world with a hurtbox on its anchor that can be struck only while exposed and a claw that is live only while it lashes, turns its lash toward the Player, looks see-through phased and solid manifest, is drawn from its sheet, and carries positional sounds; one haunts the undertow's tall stretch within sword reach (`tests/wraith_scene_tests.gd`). Playtests bait its lash, back away while it wails and strike its anchor until it is gone without being hurt, and stand in its lash to be hurt for 15.
- The Lurelight brain waits hidden with its lure lit, drifts toward a Player in sight without leaving its pool, dims when the Player comes in its jaws' reach, on the ground or in the air, snaps, lies stranded, and always follows a dim through; it sinks after stranding, or at once when struck, and stays dark a while before it lights again (`tests/lurelight_brain_tests.gd`). Its sounds follow its state changes (`tests/lurelight_sounds_tests.gd`). Its scene lies in the water with a hurtbox on its head that can be struck only while stranded and jaws that are live only as it snaps, rising 24 pixels from the surface in a wading Player's way, is drawn from its sheet, and carries positional sounds; two lurk in the pools under the undertow tunnel and two in the lake's long shallows (`tests/lurelight_scene_tests.gd`). Playtests (`tests/lurelight_playtests.gd`) charge through its dim and defeat it stranded in one blow, defeat it with two quick Attacks across two openings, and wade over it to be hurt for 15.
- The Warden brain stands dormant until the Player comes near, winds up, bashes toward the Player, rests, then slams one shockwave, hurries below half Health with shorter wind-ups and two slams, is staggered by a broken Ward until the Ward returns, and stays still and harmless once defeated (`tests/warden_brain_tests.gd`). Its scene is a Boss in the `enemy` and `boss` groups with a body, hurtbox and touch hitbox, 120 Health and 100 Experience; a dormant Warden ignores hits, a quick Attack glances off the Ward and a Charged one breaks it without costing Health, a staggered Warden pays for each hit, its touch costs 10 and its bash 15 but nothing while staggered, a slam sends a shockwave each way along the floor, a shockwave rolls then fades, and a defeated Warden kneels and reports its Experience once (`tests/nameless_warden_tests.gd`). The Warden holds the Warden's gate with a Gate shut behind it, its defeat earns the Player its Experience, records it in the Run and dissolves the Gate, and with it already defeated the Room opens without it (`tests/wardens_gate_tests.gd`); the Run remembers each Boss defeated through a defeat (`tests/run_state_tests.gd`). The Boss Health bar waits under the Pause menu, names the Boss and fills to its Health while it fights, along the bottom of the screen, and hides once it falls (`tests/boss_bar_tests.gd`). Playtests break the Ward with a real Charged Attack and land a quick one in the Stagger, and defeat the Warden, then walk through the opened Gate into Tidefen (`tests/warden_playtests.gd`).
- The Player carries its nine sounds on the `SFX` bus and plays them for a jump, a landing, a swing, a sword hit, being hurt, being defeated, reaching a Level, collecting a Supply and eating one (`tests/player_sound_scene_tests.gd`).
- The Scavenger growls on its wind-up, sounds its spear on the lunge, croaks when hurt and burbles when defeated, from any state, while recovering and patrolling stay quiet; it carries those four as positional sounds on the `SFX` bus and plays them as its brain changes state (`tests/scavenger_sounds_tests.gd`, `tests/scavenger_sound_scene_tests.gd`).
