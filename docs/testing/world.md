# Testing: The World and its Rooms

The World's data, Plan and Map; what holds for every Room; each Region, its Background and tiles; and each Room's own tests and playtests. How to run them is in [Testing](../testing.md).

- The Map centres the current Room, shows only visited Rooms, marks where the Player stands, holds the Room every Run begins in, fills each Room with its Region's colour, walls each Room with a gap at each Exit, draws faint lines between a Room's screens, and marks a Boss or Rest point on its screen; at World zoom the whole World fits the view and stands still wherever the Player is, its marks small dots and no lines between screens, its screens never smaller than 5 by 4; a World too wide for that is centred on the Player's Room, stops with its edge at the view's, and pans across, edge to edge, while it stays whole and centred the way it fits; and Near zoom centres the current Room at half the size; a pan moves the Near and Close views by screens, stops with the World's edge at the middle of the view, steps by each zoom's screens, and changes nothing at World zoom while the whole World fits; the whole World shows every Room, the planned ones in outline, and names each Region above its Rooms, each name inside the picture and clear of the others, raised a line and the picture grown upward where they would meet (`tests/map_layout_tests.gd`).
- The World map picture places each Room by its cell and size, and is just big enough to hold every Room; the Map's picture shows the World as it is, or as planned with every Planned Room (`tests/draw_world_tests.gd`).
- The World's Plan lays out every Room, built and planned, none overlapping; every planned Exit meets one back, but a Drop, which needs only a Room below; it climbs to the graveyard on the cliff tops, then down to Tidefen; the planned Undertow lies beneath the coast, reached only from Tidefen's well and the sea cave's wall; and the planned Low Canopy Jungle climbs east from Tidefen's harbour in seven Rooms to the Shrinekeeper Camp up in the canopy, reached only from Tidefen until Smuggler Inlet is planned (`tests/world_plan_tests.gd`).
- The World's data is a JSON file of Regions and Rooms, and the World answers from it, Room by Room and Region by Region (`tests/world_data_tests.gd`).
- Every Room in the World has a scene and is found in the World by it, its Region is in the World, its Exits lead to Entries in the World and are on the Map where they are (each Exit's screen and side), a Boss is marked on the Map on its screen and a Rest point on a screen with a bell, every Region has its own colour on the Map, its scene leaves the camera's limits to the Room, which keeps the camera inside its size from the World, it keeps its tiles inside its size, draws its scenery layers behind its Terrain, then its Props, its Entries and last the Player, draws only from its Region's sheets (its Terrain from the terrain sheet, every other layer from the scenery sheet), draws from the Mucho Pixels packs' sheets (all but Tidefen's bell, the coast's own prop), leaves its Background to the Room, which draws the one the World names behind all else, and its Region has a looping theme (`tests/every_room_tests.gd`). A scene the World doesn't hold plays no theme (`tests/music_tests.gd`). Each Room's own tests below keep only what is its own.
- The Rooms connect both ways along the coast, from the first Room by the palm shore, tide pools, salt cliffs, gull ledges, cliff tops, wreck graveyard and broken stair to the jungle trail, the Warden's gate and Tidefen; from the salt cliffs' hole down into the sea cave and back; and through the Undertow, from Tidefen's well down into its shaft, along the tunnel, up the temple hall and the passage to the sea cave, and from the tunnel's hole down to the pools, the dead end, and the temple stair to the lake; and off Tidefen's harbour east onto the jungle's harbour trail and up to its lower terraces (`tests/room_exit_tests.gd`).
- The first Room (`scenes/rooms/waking_coast_1.tscn`) is two screens wide, west of the palm shore on the Map, and lays out its layers and landmarks as an open surf beach with no rune props or pool, with palms on sand that nothing else stands on, and a tall jungle tree on the cliff top whose branch is the high ledge, with only the Background behind it; it keeps the plank step and the high branch one-way, starts Vael on the beach below the cliff, keeps Blacktide hidden in the sand until the Intro shows it, and walls its west edge (`tests/waking_coast_room_tests.gd`).
- The palm shore Room (`scenes/rooms/waking_coast_2.tscn`) is two screens wide, enters on the cliff top and leads east into the tide pools (`tests/waking_coast_room_2_tests.gd`); a playtest walks from the first Room into the shore and back.
- The salt cliffs Room (`scenes/rooms/waking_coast_3.tscn`, laid out in `assets/regions/waking_coast/room_layouts/waking_coast_3.aseprite`) is two screens tall: it enters on the beach level with the tide pools, keeps a Scavenger in its tidal pool, climbs one jump at a time (a jump rises two tiles, worked out from the Player's jump and gravity) up driftwood logs and wreck decks to the cliff top, leaves its highest ledge out of reach for a double jump, with a crate Cache of two Meat Pies waiting on it, leads east from the cliff top onto the gull ledges, drops through a hole under the cliff's foot into the sea cave, with a step at the hole's rim a jump below the beach for the way back up, and walls its top screen's west edge and its foot's east edge (`tests/waking_coast_room_3_tests.gd`). Playtests and climb from the beach to the cliff top with real input.
- The wreck graveyard Room (`scenes/rooms/waking_coast_4.tscn`, laid out in `assets/regions/waking_coast/room_layouts/waking_coast_4.aseprite`) is two screens wide on the cliff tops: it enters on a cliff level with the cliff tops, runs east along a beach of two tidal pools that needs none of the decks, has wreck decks that lead a jump at a time up to the stern's crate off the main way, keeps a Scavenger in its second pool, and runs its beach whole, with no way down, to its east edge, which is the Exit on to the broken stair its whole height (`tests/waking_coast_room_4_tests.gd`). Playtests walk its beach to the broken stair and back, and hop the decks up to the stern with real input. `tests/room_reach.gd` holds the reach check the Rooms use: the surfaces to stand on in a Room's Terrain, and which a walk, a single jump or a drop reaches.
- The sea cave Room (`scenes/rooms/waking_coast_5.tscn`) is two screens wide under the salt cliffs' foot: it is dropped into through an opening in its roof under the cliffs' hole and climbed out of up two one-way landings, a jump at a time, its roof otherwise whole and its west end walled; its floor runs east past a pool, a shelf a jump up holds a crate Cache of two Meat Pies, drawn from the pack's crate with its lid lifted once open (`assets/world/crate.png`), and a Breakable wall across the Shortcut shuts its east end until a Bomb breaks it or the Shortcut is opened from the Undertow (`tests/waking_coast_room_5_tests.gd`, `tests/shortcut_wall_tests.gd`). A playtest drops in from the cliffs' beach, walks east until the rock stops it short of the Exit, then climbs back out onto the step at the hole's rim and up onto the beach.
- The jungle trail Room (`scenes/rooms/waking_coast_6.tscn`, laid out in `assets/regions/waking_coast/room_layouts/waking_coast_6.aseprite`) is two screens wide: it enters at sea level, on the sand at the broken stair's foot, climbs east one tile at a time up earth steps to the heights, keeps its two-tile saplings and ferns standing on the trail, and leads east from the heights into the Warden's gate (`tests/waking_coast_room_6_tests.gd`). - The Warden's gate Room (`scenes/rooms/waking_coast_7.tscn`, laid out in `assets/regions/waking_coast/room_layouts/waking_coast_7.aseprite`) is one screen: it enters on the trail's end at the height of the jungle trail's heights, drops by a ledge into a flat, open arena that climbs back to the trail's end, and leads on east to Tidefen; the way stays open until the Nameless Warden exists (`tests/waking_coast_room_7_tests.gd`).
- The Tidefen Room (`scenes/rooms/tidefen_village_1.tscn`, laid out in `assets/regions/tidefen_village/room_layouts/tidefen_village_1.aseprite`) is six screens wide, its stilt houses, market square and harbour two each: it enters on the sand at the gate's floor height, runs an unbroken log walkway on braced stilts over its pools up from a ledge, with two plank houses on it, each with a door and a window; stands the village bell, the rest point the Map marks, in the market square by the Supply Shop's door and its stall, all walked to from the Entry; runs a plank pier on posts over the harbour's shallows, a jump up from the stone quay; hangs blue lanterns (`assets/regions/tidefen_village/props/tidefen_lantern.aseprite`, four flickering frames) under its roofs, drops through an old well in the shallows into the undertow, and past the pier steps down to a stone landing at the water's level, off which its harbour opens east onto the jungle (`tests/tidefen_village_room_1_tests.gd`).
- Tidefen's four Villagers each stand by a landmark on its floor, drawn from a sheet of their own (`assets/npcs/<role>/<role>.png`) and talking in the name of their trade: the netter in the shallows by the old well, the knot-weaver on the log walkway, the bell-keeper by the bell, and the fisher on the pier east of the bomb crate (`tests/tidefen_villager_tests.gd`). - Tidefen's bell is its Rest point (`scenes/world/rest_bell.tscn`), rung where its tile stands on the market square's sand with a `bell` Entry at its foot: ringing it refills the Player's Health and every Sub Weapon owned to 9, tolls, shows RESTED and makes it the Run's Rest point (`tests/rest_bell_tests.gd`); a defeat wakes the Player at the Rest point last rung, or on the first beach before there is one, and a new Run forgets it (`tests/run_state_tests.gd`), and a refill tops up only Sub Weapons owned (`tests/sub_weapons_tests.gd`). Played with real input, the hurt Player rings the bell with Interact and rests, then falls to a blow and wakes in Tidefen at the bell's foot with full Health (`tests/rest_playtests.gd`).
- Tidefen's Supply Shop: the Shop sells Meat Pies for 15 Gold and Brinecaps for 8, and buying spends the price and carries one more, never without the Gold or past the carry limit (`tests/shop_tests.gd`); its panel opens paused, listing each Supply with how many are carried and its price, and the Gold, a row buys one and updates them, a row the Player can't buy is greyed and says why, Back or cancel closes it, talking to the TRADER opens it, the TRADER stands at the market stall, and every Room keeps the panel above its Pause menu (`tests/shop_panel_tests.gd`). - Tidefen Village is a small Region of its own, the Safe spoke the Waking Coast and the Undertow lean on: its own Map name and colour and a calm theme that loops, the coast's sky and sea for its Background, and the coast's sheets, which a Region may share with another by naming it in the World; the Warden's gate stays the coast's last Room (`tests/tidefen_region_tests.gd`, `tests/world_data_tests.gd`).
- The Undertow is a Region of its own, drawn from the Mucho Pixels Underwater pack: its earth solid, its ledges and the temple's slabs one-way, its pools the coast's shallow water dimmed for the dark, a Background of drowned ruins in deep water whose layers scroll across but hold their height on the screen, and a looping theme of its own (`tests/undertow_region_tests.gd`).
