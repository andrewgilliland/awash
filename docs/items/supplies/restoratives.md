---
name: Restoratives
type: item_type
category: Supplies
description: Items that restore health or other expendable player resources.
---

- **Tide Draught**: restores 20 Health. Found in the Waking Coast's first caches and sold at Tidefen's Supply Shop.
- **Brinecap**: a pale, salt-crusted mushroom from the coast's caves and tunnels; restores 10 Health. Enemies forage it, and sometimes leave one where they fall (`assets/sprites/items/brinecap.aseprite`).
