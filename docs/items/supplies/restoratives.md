---
name: Restoratives
type: item_type
category: Supplies
description: Items that restore health or other expendable player resources.
---

- **Tide Draught**: restores 20 Health. Found in the Waking Coast's first caches and sold at Tidefen's Supply Shop.
- **Brinecap**: a pale, salt-crusted mushroom from the coast's caves and tunnels; restores 10 Health. Enemies forage it, and sometimes leave one where they fall (`assets/items/brinecap.aseprite`). Scavengers and Storm Wisps drop it.
- **Glowroe**: a clutch of faintly glowing roe from the Undertow's pools; restores 20 Health. A defeated Lurelight sometimes leaves one (`assets/items/glowroe.aseprite`).
- **Mourning Salt**: salt wept from a Wraith's robes; restores half of Vael's max Health, so it keeps its worth as he grows. A defeated Wraith sometimes leaves one (`assets/items/mourning_salt.aseprite`).
