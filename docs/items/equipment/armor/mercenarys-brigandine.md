Found Armor of the Blackreef Approach: Defense 8, and its Armor trait, defeating an enemy restores 3 Health. Like every found Armor, it gives less plain Defense than the Wayfarer Leathers once a Smith has upgraded them for its part of the game (Reinforced Leathers, 5, until the Ironwake Smith; Runed Leathers, 10, after), and no Smith upgrades it. Health it restores never goes past Maximum Health.
