Found Armor of the Blackreef Castle: Defense 10, and its Armor trait, once per Rest, a killing hit leaves 1 Health instead. Like every Armor, it is found as it is and never upgraded. Each Region's find gives a little more Defense than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth wearing. Resting at a Rest point readies it again. It is the Oathblade's kin: the same oathkeepers, the same rune light.
