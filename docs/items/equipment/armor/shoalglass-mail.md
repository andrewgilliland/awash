Found Armor of the Northern Reaches: Defense 6, and its Armor trait, supplies restore 50% more Health. Like every Armor, it is found as it is and never upgraded. Each Region's find gives a little more Defense than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth wearing. A share of a Supply's Health, such as a Glowroe's half, grows by half as well.
