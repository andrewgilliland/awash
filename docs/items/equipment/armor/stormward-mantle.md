Found Armor of the Stormwatch Tower: Defense 4, and its Armor trait, projectile hits cost 5 less. Like every found Armor, it gives less plain Defense than the Wayfarer Leathers once a Smith has upgraded them for its part of the game (Reinforced Leathers, 5, until the Ironwake Smith; Runed Leathers, 10, after), and no Smith upgrades it. Made for the tower's Wardbreakers and storm-flung shards.
