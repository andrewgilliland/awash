Found Armor of the Underroot: Defense 4, and its Armor trait, touching an enemy's body takes 5 less of Vael's Health. Like every Armor, it is found as it is and never upgraded. Each Region's find gives a little more Defense than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth wearing. Made for the cave floors, where burrowers come up underfoot and there is no room to step aside.
