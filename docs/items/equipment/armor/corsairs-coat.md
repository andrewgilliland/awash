Found Armor of the Low Canopy Jungle: Defense 2, and its Armor trait, defeated enemies give 50% more Gold. Like every Armor, it is found as it is and never upgraded. Each Region's find gives a little more Defense than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth wearing. Its Defense sits low on purpose: it is the Armor to swap on for gathering Gold, not for a hard fight.
