Every Run starts in the [Worn Leather Tunic](armor/worn-leather-tunic.md), which gives Defense 0. Every other Armor is **found Armor**, one for each major Region, in a Cache down a Branch off the Main path. None is sold or dropped by a Boss, and no Armor is ever upgraded: each is found as it is.

Each found Armor gives some Defense and one Armor trait, an upside of its own that takes nothing away. Each Region's find gives a little more Defense than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth wearing. The Corsair's Coat sits low on purpose, as the Armor for gathering Gold. Wearing an Armor changes only its icon and the Equipment Panel, not Vael's sprite.

A found Armor's in-game description is two lines at most and ends with its Defense and its trait. That is the only place the trait shows in the game. Each Armor is built with its Region, once the systems its trait needs exist, as named in each doc's `needs`.
