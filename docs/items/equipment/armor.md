There are two kinds of Armor:

- **The Wayfarer Leathers** are what Vael wakes in. The Smiths upgrade them: the Worn Leather Tunic (0), then Reinforced Leathers (5) at Emberroot, then Runed Leathers (10) at Ironwake.
- **Found Armor** is one Armor for each major Region, in a Cache down a Branch off the Main path. None is sold, dropped by a Boss, or upgraded. Each gives some Defense and one Armor trait, an upside of its own. None takes anything away.

Once upgraded, the Leathers always give more plain Defense than any found Armor of that part of the game. Choosing a found Armor means picking its trait over a few points of Defense. Wearing an Armor changes only its icon and the Equipment Panel, not Vael's sprite.

A found Armor's in-game description is two lines at most and ends with its Defense and its trait. That is the only place the trait shows in the game. Each Armor is built with its Region, once the systems its trait needs exist, as named in each doc's `needs`.
