Blacktide is the Weapon the Smiths upgrade: Recovered Blacktide (Attack 3), then Reforged Blacktide (12) at Emberroot, then Awakened Blacktide (25) at Ironwake. Recovered Blacktide gives less Attack than the Oathblade, but it is the only Weapon whose quick slashes break a Ward. Once upgraded, it gives more plain Attack than any found Weapon of that part of the game, so a found Weapon is held for its Weapon trait. It is not built yet.
