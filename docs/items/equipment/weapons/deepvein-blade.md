Found Weapon of the Underroot: Attack 10, and its Weapon trait, defeated enemies give 50% more Experience. Like every found Weapon, it gives less plain Attack than Blacktide once a Smith has upgraded it for its part of the game (Reforged Blacktide, 12, until the Ironwake Smith; Awakened Blacktide, 25, after), and no Smith upgrades it. It swings like every Weapon, with the same reach and timing, and it has its own held sprite and Sword Beam. It is the Weapon to hold for gaining Levels, as the Corsair's Coat is the Armor for Gold.
