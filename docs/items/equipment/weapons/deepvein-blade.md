Found Weapon of the Underroot: Attack 12, and its Weapon trait, defeated enemies give 50% more Experience. Like every Weapon, it is found as it is and never upgraded. Each Region's find gives a little more Attack than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth holding. It swings like every Weapon, with the same reach and timing, and it has its own held sprite and Sword Beam. It is the Weapon to hold for gaining Levels, as the Corsair's Coat is the Armor for Gold.
