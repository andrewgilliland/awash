Found Weapon of the Stormwatch Tower: Attack 11, and its Weapon trait, its Sword Beam leaps to one more enemy nearby. Like every found Weapon, it gives less plain Attack than Blacktide once a Smith has upgraded it for its part of the game (Reforged Blacktide, 12, until the Ironwake Smith; Awakened Blacktide, 25, after), and no Smith upgrades it. It swings like every Weapon, with the same reach and timing, and it has its own held sprite and Sword Beam. The leap costs what the Sword Beam does, and it never comes back to the enemy it left.
