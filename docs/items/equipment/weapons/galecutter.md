Found Weapon of the Northern Reaches: Attack 16, and its Weapon trait, a slash that lands while Vael is jumping or falling takes 50% more of the enemy's Health. Like every Weapon, it is found as it is and never upgraded. Each Region's find gives a little more Attack than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth holding. It swings like every Weapon, with the same reach and timing, and it has its own held sprite and Sword Beam. A slash counts as in the air whenever the Player is jumping or falling when it lands.
