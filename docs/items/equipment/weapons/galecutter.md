Found Weapon of the Northern Reaches: Attack 10, and its Weapon trait, slashes in the air cost 50% more. Like every found Weapon, it gives less plain Attack than Blacktide once a Smith has upgraded it for its part of the game (Reforged Blacktide, 12, until the Ironwake Smith; Awakened Blacktide, 25, after), and no Smith upgrades it. It swings like every Weapon, with the same reach and timing, and it has its own held sprite and Sword Beam. A slash counts as in the air whenever the Player is jumping or falling when it lands.
