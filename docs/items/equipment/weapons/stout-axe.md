The Undertow's found Weapon, in the pools' chest. Its quick slashes chop: one breaks a Shortcut's cracked wall, as only a Charged Attack does with any other Weapon. A Breakable wall, like the sea cave's rock, still takes a Bomb. Its Charged Attack looses an Axe Throw in place of a Sword Beam: a glowing copy of the axe, spinning three turns a second, thrown up and out along Vael's facing to fall in an arc. It strikes the first thing in its way, charged, for twice what a quick slash costs, and stops there or at the world's terrain. It swings like every Weapon, with the same reach and timing.
