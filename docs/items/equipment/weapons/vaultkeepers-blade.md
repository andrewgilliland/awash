Found Weapon of the Blackreef Castle: Attack 22, and its Weapon trait, each hit in a row costs 5 more, up to 20 more, until the Player is hit. Like every found Weapon, it gives less plain Attack than Blacktide once a Smith has upgraded it for its part of the game (Reforged Blacktide, 12, until the Ironwake Smith; Awakened Blacktide, 25, after), and no Smith upgrades it. It swings like every Weapon, with the same reach and timing, and it has its own held sprite and Sword Beam. Being hit, or leaving the Room, ends the streak.
