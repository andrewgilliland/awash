Found Weapon of the Blackreef Castle: Attack 25, and its Weapon trait, each hit in a row costs 5 more, up to 20 more, until the Player is hit. Like every Weapon, it is found as it is and never upgraded. Each Region's find gives a little more Attack than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth holding. It swings like every Weapon, with the same reach and timing, and it has its own held sprite and Sword Beam. Being hit, or leaving the Room, ends the streak.
