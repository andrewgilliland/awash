Found Weapon of the Blackreef Approach: Attack 22, and its Weapon trait, hits on a Boss cost 25% more. Like every Weapon, it is found as it is and never upgraded. Each Region's find gives a little more Attack than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth holding. It swings like every Weapon, with the same reach and timing, and it has its own held sprite and Sword Beam. Only a Boss counts, not the enemies a Boss calls in.
