Every Run starts with the [Short Sword](weapons/short-sword.md), Attack 0, from Mara. Every other Weapon is found, and no Weapon is ever upgraded: each is found as it is.

- **Blacktide** is the legendary Weapon, in a wreck on the Waking Coast that opens once the Nameless Warden is defeated. It gives Attack 8, and its quick slashes break a Ward. No other Weapon's quick slashes do.
- **Found Weapon** is one Weapon for each major Region, in a Cache down a Branch off the Main path. The Oathblade is the exception, since a Wraith drops it. None is sold. Each gives some Attack and one Weapon trait, an upside of its own that takes nothing away.

Each Region's find gives a little more Attack than the last, and its trait is what keeps an older one worth holding. Every Weapon is a one-handed blade with the same swing, reach and timing, and only its Attack, trait, held sprite and Sword Beam differ. A Charged Attack's Sword Beam costs twice a quick slash for every Weapon, with any trait that makes a hit cost more counted in.

A found Weapon's in-game description is two lines at most and ends with its Attack and its trait. That is the only place the trait shows in the game. Each Weapon is built with its Region, once the systems its trait needs exist, as named in each doc's `needs`.
