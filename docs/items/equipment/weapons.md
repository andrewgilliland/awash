There are three kinds of Weapon:

- **The Short Sword** is what every Run starts with: Attack 0, from Mara.
- **Blacktide** is the legendary Weapon the Smiths upgrade. It goes from Recovered Blacktide (3) to Reforged Blacktide (12) at Emberroot, then Awakened Blacktide (25) at Ironwake. Its quick slashes break a Ward, and no other Weapon's do.
- **Found Weapon** is one Weapon for each major Region, in a Cache down a Branch off the Main path. The Oathblade is the exception, since a Wraith drops it. None is sold or upgraded. Each gives some Attack and one Weapon trait, an upside of its own that takes nothing away.

Once upgraded, Blacktide always gives more plain Attack than any found Weapon of that part of the game. Choosing a found Weapon means picking its trait over a few points of Attack. Every Weapon is a one-handed blade with the same swing, reach and timing, and only its Attack, trait, held sprite and Sword Beam differ. A Charged Attack's Sword Beam costs twice a quick slash for every Weapon, with any trait that makes a hit cost more counted in.

A found Weapon's in-game description is two lines at most and ends with its Attack and its trait. That is the only place the trait shows in the game. Each Weapon is built with its Region, once the systems its trait needs exist, as named in each doc's `needs`.
