## Description

Once the ceremonial landing site for wardens sworn to protect forbidden knowledge, the Waking Coast is where all oaths began. During the Memory Tempests, the tide carried shattered runes back to shore, and each storm stripped names from their carvings. The fisher-families of nearby Tidefen abandoned deep water after seeing lights beneath the waves and became practical salvagers and mapmakers, preserving identity through memory-knot rituals and strict bell curfews. Vael's unlikely survival of the black surf and the arrival of Ilyr's scouts make him seem like either a fortunate castaway or a dangerous omen.

## Level Mechanics

### Traversal

- Teach walking, running, and jumping across wreckage, tidal pools, one-way platforms, and salt-slick ledges.
- Use shallow water and rising tides to encourage steady forward movement without making the route lethal.
- Introduce short drops that loop back to the main path and demonstrate how the region reconnects.
- Show an elevated double-jump gate early so the player knows there is a reason to return later.

### Combat

- Use Scavengers to teach normal melee attacks and guarding against direct lunges.
- Use Storm Wisps to teach Throwing Knives against enemies that are difficult to reach with Blacktide.
- Use Wraiths to teach observation by exposing their rune anchors between phasing attacks.
- Place Throwing Knife caches before ranged encounters and allow defeated enemies to replenish a limited supply.
- Introduce minor magical barriers that Blacktide can cut with Special Attack before the boss uses a stronger ward.

### Exploration

- Hide Supplies in optional wreck compartments, caves, and ledges visible from the main route. The first two caches, a Tide Draught each, are the wreck graveyard's stern compartment and the sea cave's shelf.
- Use broken ships and runes as landmarks so players can orient without a detailed map.
- Place the first rest point and Supply Shop in Tidefen Village at the end of the region. Ringing Tidefen's bell is the rest point.

### Backtracking

- Keep the first visit completable with starting movement abilities.
- Reserve elevated wrecks and hidden rune alcoves for the later double-jump return. Double jump opens two gates here: the crate ledge above the salt cliffs, up to the rune alcove and its Memory Relic, and Tidefen's rooftops, which hold a cache.
- Let the return route reconnect to Tidefen through a short one-way drop. Tidefen's well drops into the undertow, a shortcut back west to the sea cave; it stays shut from the cave side until first used from Tidefen.

### Boss Test

- The Reefback, a great sea-ape crusted with coral, shows each attack with a Tell before it comes: from afar a knuckle charge, a leap slam or a thrown chunk of reef; up close a lunge swipe or a ground pound.
- Moving on the Tell answers the charge, leap and throw, jumping clears the pound's shockwaves, and a Charged Attack breaks its Ward for a Stagger.
- At half Health it beats its chest and brings rubble down on marked spots, then hurries.
