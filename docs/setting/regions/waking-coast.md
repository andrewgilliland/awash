## Description

Once the ceremonial landing site for wardens sworn to protect forbidden knowledge, the Waking Coast is where all oaths began. During the Memory Tempests, the tide carried shattered oath-stones back to shore, and each storm stripped names from their carvings. The fisher-families of nearby Tidefen abandoned deep water after seeing lights beneath the waves and became practical salvagers and mapmakers, preserving identity through memory-knot rituals and strict bell curfews. Vael's unlikely survival of the black surf and the arrival of Ilyr's scouts make him seem like either a fortunate castaway or a dangerous omen.

## Level Mechanics

### Traversal

- Teach walking, running, and jumping across wreckage, tidal pools, one-way platforms, and salt-slick ledges.
- Use shallow water and rising tides to encourage steady forward movement without making the route lethal.
- Introduce short drops that loop back to the main path and demonstrate how the region reconnects.
- Show an elevated double-jump gate early so the player knows there is a reason to return later.

### Combat

- Use Scavengers to teach normal melee attacks and guarding against direct lunges.
- Use Storm Wisps to teach Throwing Knives against enemies that are difficult to reach with Blacktide.
- Use Wraiths to teach observation by exposing their oath-stone anchors between phasing attacks.
- Place Throwing Knife caches before ranged encounters and allow defeated enemies to replenish a limited supply.
- Introduce minor magical barriers that Blacktide can cut with Special Attack before the boss uses a stronger ward.

### Exploration

- Hide Supplies in optional wreck compartments, caves, and ledges visible from the main route.
- Use broken ships and oath-stones as landmarks so players can orient without a detailed map.
- Place the first rest point and Supply Shop in Tidefen Village at the end of the region.

### Backtracking

- Keep the first visit completable with starting movement abilities.
- Reserve elevated wrecks and hidden oath-stone alcoves for the later double-jump return.
- Let the return route reconnect to Tidefen through a short one-way drop.

### Boss Test

- The Nameless Warden combines shield counters, ground shockwaves, and a magical guard.
- Guarding answers its predictable strikes, jumping avoids its shockwaves, and Special Attack breaks its ward.
- Throwing Knives provide a safe option during brief periods when the Warden controls the ground nearby.
