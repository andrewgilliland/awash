# PROTOTYPE: the Low Canopy Jungle's layout (throwaway)

Question: how should the jungle's seven Rooms and the Shrinekeeper Camp sit on the World's grid?

`variants.py` writes one of three layouts into a copy of `assets/world.json`; `draw_map.sh --plan` drew each PNG.

- A, Ascent: a steady staircase east and up from Tidefen's harbour. **Chosen 2026-11-08.**
- B, Over the village: up off the harbour, back west above Tidefen, then east along the top.
- C, Low then high: flat at sea level, then one tall climb.

Answer: A. The jungle climbs steadily east and up from Tidefen's harbour: harbour trail, lower terraces (the Spore Beast's lair below them), canopy walk, upper terraces, shrine steps, the Shrinekeeper Camp, then the root hollow down toward Smuggler Inlet. B was first picked, then changed to A the same day.
