# Equipment

**Equipment**:
What the Player wears or wields, one piece in each slot, kept for the whole Run rather than spent like a Supply. A defeat keeps it.
_Avoid_: Gear, outfit, loadout

**Weapon**:
The Equipment slot for what the Player wields: the sword each Attack swings, drawn in the Player's hand and in its Sword Beam. It is never empty; a new Run holds the Short Sword, and the Equipment Panel takes up any other Weapon owned.
_Avoid_: Blade, arm, main hand

**Short Sword**:
The Weapon Mara pulls from the sand on the Waking Coast and hands to Vael: a plain blade with Attack 0.
_Avoid_: Starting weapon, default weapon

**Oathblade**:
A ceremonial blade from the oathkeepers the Wraiths once were: a Weapon with Attack 5. A defeated Wraith sometimes leaves it where it fell, until the Player owns it; touching it takes it up at once.
_Avoid_: Wraith sword, ghost blade

**Attack** (of a Weapon):
What the Weapon held adds to each hit it lands, on top of the Player's Strength. Written `ATK` in the Equipment Panel.
_Avoid_: Damage, power, weapon strength

**Sub Weapon**:
The Equipment slot for what Ranged Attack throws, chosen in the Equipment Panel from the Sub Weapons the Player owns, or left empty. A new Run owns none. Unlike a Weapon, it is spent: the Player carries up to 9 of each, shown with its icon under the Health bar while it is held, and a defeat does not take them away. Resting at a Rest point refills every one owned to 9.
_Avoid_: Secondary weapon, subweapon, ammo (as the slot)

**Bomb**:
A Tidefen fisher's clay Bomb: a Sub Weapon. Lobbed along the Player's facing, it bounces once and rests while its fuse sputters for a second, then bursts, striking every enemy within 24 pixels for 20, as a charged hit, so it breaks what a Sword Beam breaks, and as a blast, so it breaks a Breakable wall. Its blast never harms the Player. The bomb crate on Tidefen's pier, a Cache, holds the first 5.
_Avoid_: Grenade, explosive, powder keg

**Armor**:
The Equipment slot for what the Player wears, chosen in the Equipment Panel from the Armor the Player owns, or left empty for no Defense. A new Run owns and wears the Worn Leather Tunic; the Smiths upgrade it into the rest of the Wayfarer Leathers. More is found: one found Armor per major Region, each in a Cache, such as the Scale Mail.
_Avoid_: Armour, clothing, outfit

**Defense**:
How much Health the Player's Armor takes off each hit that lands. A hit always costs at least 5.
_Avoid_: AC, armor class, resistance, guard

**Armor trait**:
The one upside a found Armor gives besides its Defense, such as more Gold from defeated enemies. It never takes anything away. The Wayfarer Leathers have none; they give more plain Defense instead.
_Avoid_: Perk, bonus, passive, set bonus

**Found Armor**:
Armor found in a Cache down a Branch, one per major Region, rather than worn from the start. No Smith upgrades it, and each gives Defense and an Armor trait.
_Avoid_: Loot armor, rare armor

**Wayfarer Leathers**:
The Armor Vael wakes in, which the Smiths upgrade: the Worn Leather Tunic (Defense 0), then Reinforced Leathers (5) at Emberroot, then Runed Leathers (10) at Ironwake. Only the Worn Leather Tunic is in the game so far.
_Avoid_: Starting armor set, leather armor

**Worn Leather Tunic**:
The Armor Vael wakes in on the Waking Coast: sea-weathered leather that gives no Defense yet.
_Avoid_: Saltworn Leathers, starting armor, default armor

**Scale Mail**:
Armor found in the Undertow wreck's chest: a drowned warden's scale mail, green with rust, giving 3 Defense. Taken up, it is owned and worn at once. Its planned Armor trait, not built yet: hits push the Player back half as far.
_Avoid_: Drowned Scale Mail, chain mail, scale armor

**Corsair's Coat**:
Planned found Armor of the Low Canopy Jungle, in a pirate stash in Smuggler Inlet: Defense 2. Its Armor trait is that defeated enemies give 50% more Gold.
_Avoid_: Pirate coat, Gold coat

**Cinderhide**:
Planned found Armor of the Underroot, in a vent cave: hide cured hard over Emberroot's vents, giving 4 Defense. Its Armor trait is that touching an enemy's body costs 5 less.
_Avoid_: Ember armor, Emberplate, cinder mail

**Courier's Silks**:
Planned found Armor of the High Canopy, in a nest in the Canopy Shaft: Defense 3. Its Armor trait is that the double jump goes higher.
_Avoid_: Courier armor, silk armor, canopy silks

**Shoalglass Mail**:
Planned found Armor of the Northern Reaches, on a sunk causeway span in the North Shoals: Defense 4. Its Armor trait is that Supplies restore 50% more Health.
_Avoid_: Glass armor, Glassmire mail, sea-glass mail

**Stormward Mantle**:
Planned found Armor of Stormwatch Tower, in a weather engine room: Defense 4. Its Armor trait is that Projectile hits cost 5 less.
_Avoid_: Storm cloak, copper mantle

**Mercenary's Brigandine**:
Planned found Armor of the Blackreef Approach, in an abandoned mercenary camp: Defense 8. Its Armor trait is that defeating an enemy restores 3 Health.
_Avoid_: Brigandine, mercenary armor, riveted coat

**Oathkeeper's Plate**:
Planned found Armor of Blackreef Castle, in a sealed chapel off the Doctrine halls: Defense 9. Its Armor trait is that, once per Rest, a hit that would defeat the Player leaves 1 Health instead.
_Avoid_: Oath plate, warden plate, last stand
