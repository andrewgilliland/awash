# Menus

**Dialogue box**:
Shows a conversation one line at a time, typed out, with the speaker's name; a press finishes a line or moves to the next. The Player is held still while it is open. It sits at the foot of the screen, or at the top when the foot would cover the Player or whoever is speaking, and keeps that side until the conversation ends.
_Avoid_: Text box, speech bubble, message window

**Start screen**:
Where the game opens: the AWASH logo over the coast, its looping theme, and a PRESS START prompt that opens START and QUIT, with CONTINUE first when there is a Saved Run. CONTINUE takes up the Saved Run; START begins a new Run, asking first whether to lose the Saved Run when there is one.
_Avoid_: Title screen, main menu, splash

**Pause menu**:
Pauses play and offers Resume, Quit and its Panels. Quit saves the Run before it ends the game.
_Avoid_: Options menu, start menu

**Panel**:
One page of the Pause menu, shown from when its menu button takes focus until another one does. Left and right move from Panel to Panel, round from the last to the first, and the Pause menu opens on the first.
_Avoid_: Tab, screen, page

**Inventory**:
The Panel listing each kind of Supply the Player carries and how many. Choosing one there uses it, with play paused: a Restorative restores its Health, and nothing is spent while Health is full.
_Avoid_: Supplies Panel, items menu, bag

**Equipment Panel**:
The Panel showing each Equipment slot with its piece above a divider, and below it the pieces owned for the slot last focused, the one equipped marked. Choosing a slot moves into its pieces; choosing one equips it, and None empties a slot that can be empty. Attack and Defense show above the divider, each with what it would become while a Weapon, or an Armor, is focused.
_Avoid_: Equip screen, gear menu, loadout

**Description**:
A line or two at the foot of the Inventory and the Equipment Panel saying what the focused Supply, or piece of Equipment, is. Each Supply and piece has its own.
_Avoid_: Tooltip, flavor text, help text

**Map**:
The Panel that draws each visited Room as a block, sized in screens and filled with its Region's colour, walled round with a gap at each Exit, marking any Rest point or Boss, with the name of the Region or Safe spoke the Player is in and a dot where the Player stands. Unvisited Rooms stay hidden, though a visited Room's Exits show the way on. It has three zooms: World, the whole World at once, as large as fits, where a Rest point or Boss is a dot in its colour; and Near and Close, centred on the Player. It opens on World; Jump zooms in and Attack out while its tab has focus, and it keeps the zoom chosen until the game is quit. Interact on its tab starts exploring it, framed while it lasts: the directions pan Near and Close, as far as the World's edge at the middle of the view, instead of moving the menu on, and Interact again stops. Zooming keeps the spot in the middle, and the Map opens centred on the Player again each time the menu does. Its LEGEND button, under it, opens the Map's legend over it: the Map's marks, a swatch for each Region the Player has visited, and the Map's keys; the button again, Interact or Back closes it. The Map key opens the Pause menu on the Map, and closes it from there. Drawn for the whole World at once, as it is or as the Plan lays it out with Planned Rooms in outline, it is how the World's shape is seen before it is built.
_Avoid_: Minimap, world map, atlas
